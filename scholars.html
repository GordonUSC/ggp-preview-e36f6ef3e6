How to apply

Applications open once a year.

  1. Check you are eligibleYou are an undergraduate or graduate student building toward a career in games, and you show up for an underestimated community. To confirmExact eligibility wording, with Alex Rogers.
  2. Gather what you needA short essay about what you make and who you show up for.
  3. ApplyThis year's Scholars are already selected. Leave your email and we will send the link when the next application opens. To confirmThe month applications open.
