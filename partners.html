Partners in Pride, and what they made possible

  • Take-Two Interactive, with 2K and ZyngaSupports GGP Scholars, hosts the 2K Studio Tour for Scholars at GDC (2024, 2025 and 2026), and made the 2022 film series Five Stories About Everyone with GGP.
  • Wizards of the CoastZero Proof Board Game Night, every year. Its Dungeon Masters and Magic coaches teach newcomers at the tables.
  • Raw ThrillsCo-presenter of the Collegiate Games Challenge and its $90,000 in prizes in 2025.
  • ESA FoundationA joint LGBTQ+ Service Scholarship with GGP since 2019.
  • NYU, Carnegie Mellon and USCAcademic partners of CYSTEM Scholars since 2022.
  • UnityGame jam workshops and tips, and a campus tour for Scholars in 2024.
  • Epic GamesAn Unreal Editor for Fortnite workshop for Global Game Jam in 2025.
  • GDC Festival of GamingFestival passes for Scholars and the room for the Scholars Breakfast.
  • ModulateSupported the 2nd Annual Zero Proof Board Game Night in 2025.
  • Stern PinballBrought a pinball machine to Zero Proof Board Game Night in 2025.

SourcedFrom GGP's records and public pages, read November 17, 2026. Each partner confirms its listing before launch.
