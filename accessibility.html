Accessibility and privacy

Built so everyone can use it.

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. If something gets in your way, tell us and we will fix it.

What we do

  • You can zoom. Nothing blocks pinch-to-zoom.
  • Every page has a skip link, visible focus states and full keyboard navigation.
  • Body text meets at least 4.5 to 1 contrast. Color is never the only signal.
  • Images that carry meaning have alt text. Decorative illustrations are marked as decoration.
  • Motion is short and subtle, and stops if your device asks for reduced motion. Video is muted and can be paused. Sound is off until you ask for it.
  • Interactive features, like the cohort map and the program filters, also work as plain lists without JavaScript.

Contact: ggpcharity@gmail.com

Privacy

  • Scholar profiles are opt-in and controlled by the Scholar. Every field can be public, recruiter-only or hidden.
  • We never out anyone. No profile is created from an application, Discord, an event or a donation record.
  • Cohort data is published in aggregate only. Groups under three are merged or left out.
  • Minimal, privacy-respecting analytics. No third-party ad pixels.
  • Donor names are shown only with explicit permission.
